Create your calorie counter.

Track the energy value of foods and beverages consumed to manage weight (loss, gain, or maintenance), monitor portion sizes, and ensure a balanced intake of macronutrients.

Weight Management

By calculating your daily caloric needs (based on age, sex, weight, and activity level), a counter helps you create a calorie deficit for weight loss or a surplus for weight gain.

Awareness of Eating Habits

It helps identify hidden calories and problem areas—like overeating certain foods or consuming too many liquid calories

Nutritional balance

It tracks macronutrients (Carbohydrates, Protein, Fat) and micronutrients to ensure you are meeting your body's needs.
